Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

toEfu is a free, non-commercial English-learning website run by one person as a personal project. This page explains what we store, who can see it, and how to delete it.

toEfu 是個人經營、免費、非商業的英文學習網站。本頁說明我們儲存哪些資料、誰看得到，以及如何刪除。

The vocabulary quiz (/quiz) records your answers anonymously, with no name or account attached. We use them for rankings and to improve the questions. Some players are also asked, before the score appears, how they chose one or two of their answers (for example "I was sure" or "I guessed"). Answering is optional, and the reply is stored the same anonymous way.

測驗會匿名記錄作答結果，用於排名與改善題目。部分作答者在成績公布前，可能會被問到其中一兩題是怎麼選的（例如「很確定」或「用猜的」），可以不回答；回答同樣是匿名記錄。

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers. There are no ads. The only analytics is on the quiz page (/quiz), described under Services we use.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

我們不販售資料、沒有廣告。唯一的使用統計在測驗頁（/quiz），詳見下方「Services we use」。你的 Email 不會顯示給其他使用者。

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, App Check) — stores the data above. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Firebase Analytics (quiz page only) — counts anonymous steps such as starting or finishing the quiz, to see where people drop off. It is not used on the rest of the site and is not linked to your account.
  • Google reCAPTCHA (through App Check, if turned on) — checks that requests come from this website, not from bots.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website files. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses).

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. Notification tokens that stop working are removed automatically.

Delete your data

  • Delete your account: Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Delete my account. This removes your threads, replies, likes, reposts, saved words, profile, notification settings and your sign-in. It can't be undone.
  • Delete a single thread or reply: use the trash / Delete button on it.
  • Without an account: Settings → Delete data on this device, or clear this site's data in your browser.

刪除帳號：個人主頁 → ⚙️ Settings → Delete my account，會刪除你的串文、留言、按讚、轉發、收藏、個人資料和登入帳號，無法復原。

Children

toEfu is meant for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please don't create an account.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/chiaying1230/toefl-threads/issues (please don't post personal information there).

Changes
